Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok Auto Publisher handles information when you use the desktop application.

1. Information processed

The application may process TikTok account information returned through TikTok's authorization and creator information APIs, such as your TikTok user identifier, username, display name, profile image URL, and available publishing settings.

2. Access tokens

Authentication tokens are used to communicate with TikTok on your behalf. The application is designed to store authentication credentials locally and securely rather than storing your TikTok password.

3. Video files

Videos selected for publishing are read from the local folder configured by you and sent to TikTok when you initiate or authorize a publishing action. The application does not intentionally sell or share your video files with unrelated third parties.

4. Third-party service

TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms. Your use of TikTok features remains subject to TikTok's policies.

5. Data retention

Local application data may remain on your computer until you remove it. You may disconnect your TikTok account and delete local application data using the application's available controls.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored application credentials and data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the application operator using the contact information provided with the application.
